tf-workのStatefulSetをデプロイしました。

Podのシェルに入る:
  kubectl exec -it {{ include "tf-work.fullname" . }}-0 -- bash
  (OpenShiftの場合: oc rsh {{ include "tf-work.fullname" . }}-0)

ローカルの内容をPodに反映する:
  kubectl cp ./ {{ include "tf-work.fullname" . }}-0:/workspace
  (OpenShiftの場合: oc rsync ./ {{ include "tf-work.fullname" . }}-0:/workspace --exclude=.git --exclude=.terraform)
